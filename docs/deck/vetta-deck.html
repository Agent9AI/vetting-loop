The problem

Kenya's Parliament vets every Cabinet Secretary and Principal Secretary before they take office. Across the 2022 Cabinet, the 2022 PS slate and the 2024 reconstitution, the gate said yes 92 times.

What the record shows

A mass dismissal that returned half the cabinet.

On 11 July 2024, during the Finance Bill protests, the President dismissed the cabinet. Four weeks later, Parliament approved its replacement. The pattern only appears when appointment histories are linked per person.

What VETTA does

One durable, citable record for every public appointment.

Dossier

Source-linked appointment history for every nominee. A public question queue, ranked by citizens.

Hearing record

Defamation safety by design
"A record with no signals is not an endorsement.
A record with signals is not an accusation."
  • No bare allegations. Every flag cites a source document, and rejection grounds are recorded verbatim.
  • Process, not persons. Signals describe how an appointment moved through the gate.
  • Corrections are first-class. A secure reporting channel, and corrections replace the old record everywhere it is mirrored.
  • Experimental scoring stays gated. The Integrity Index runs on synthetic data, stamped MOCK and pseudonymised for any public deployment.
